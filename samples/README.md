# Sample Index

This directory contains the index for the public preview sample associated with the First-Person Perspective Driving Video Collection.

## Source Dataset

- Product: First-Person Perspective Driving Video Collection
- Product ID: `VID-20260728-151612-899`
- DataMall: https://datamall.thordata.com/datasets/VID-20260728-151612-899
- Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/datasets/thordata/first-person-driving-video-v1

## Current Sample

The public preview contains one first-person driving video with coarse scene-segment annotations.

The sample includes scenes such as:

- Roadside service area or roadside stop
- Open mountain road
- Valley road
- Curving mountain road
- Road with a vehicle ahead

This repository does not contain the complete commercial dataset.

The sample index and metadata describe the public preview only. They do not grant additional rights to download, copy, modify, train on, evaluate, or redistribute the source media beyond the applicable sample terms.

For complete data access and commercial licensing, visit the [Thordata DataMall product page](https://datamall.thordata.com/datasets/VID-20260728-151612-899).

## Annotation Level

The current annotations are segment-level scene descriptions.

They are not lane-level, object-level, or frame-accurate autonomous-driving annotations.

## Responsible Use

Do not use the sample to identify, track, or profile individuals.

Before using or redistributing the sample, review it for faces, voices, license plates, GPS information, addresses, and other sensitive information.

For usage or licensing questions, contact the [Thordata team](https://www.thordata.com/contact-us).
