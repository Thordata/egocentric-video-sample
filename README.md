# First-Person Driving Video Sample

Public documentation and sample access information for first-person driving video data.

This repository is associated with the [First-Person Perspective Driving Video Collection](https://datamall.thordata.com/datasets/VID-20260728-151612-899) available through Thordata DataMall.

## Dataset

The dataset contains driver-seat perspective video showing scenes such as:

- Roadside service areas
- Open mountain roads
- Valley roads
- Curving roads
- Vehicles ahead
- Daily driving and scene-understanding scenarios

## Repository Scope

This repository documents a public preview sample and its associated metadata.

The current public sample is hosted on Hugging Face:

[First-Person Driving Video v1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/thordata/first-person-driving-video-v1)

The complete commercial dataset is not included in this repository.

This repository is dedicated to the First-Person Perspective Driving Video Collection and does not include unrelated datasets or video products.

## Intended Use

The sample is intended to support:

- First-person video understanding
- Computer vision research
- Scene understanding
- Autonomous-driving research
- Multimodal AI and vision-language model prototyping
- Video retrieval workflows
- Dataset structure and metadata evaluation

## Public Sample

The public preview currently includes one first-person driving video sample with scene-segment annotations.

The sample covers sections such as:

- Roadside service area or roadside stop
- Departure and open mountain road
- Valley road
- Curving mountain road
- Road with a vehicle ahead

The sample is not the complete commercial dataset and should not be treated as a representative release of all available data.

## Annotations

The current public preview includes coarse scene-segment annotations.

These annotations describe visible scene context and driving events. They are not lane-level, object-level, or frame-accurate autonomous-driving annotations.

Any derived frame metadata or segment labels should be treated as reviewable annotations unless a separate annotation review confirms their accuracy.
